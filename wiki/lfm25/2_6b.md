# LFM2.5-2.6B Architecture & Execution Specification

This document provides the complete architectural breakdown, mathematical execution pipeline, tensor inventory, and memory budget for **LFM2.5-2.6B** within the [`tuf`](file:///home/nii/workspace/tuf) inference runtime.

---

## 1. Model Overview & Hyperparameters

* **Model File**: `LiquidAI/LFM2.5-2.6B/LFM2.5-2.6B-Q4_K_M.gguf`
* **File Size**: **1.56 GiB** (1,674,454,848 bytes on disk)
* **Architecture**: `lfm2` (Dense Hybrid Conv1D + GQA Attention)
* **Total Layers**: **30 blocks**
* **Active Parameters**: 2.6 Billion (100% active per token)
* **Embedding Dimension ($d$)**: 2048
* **Context Length**: 128,000 tokens
* **Vocabulary Size**: 128,000 tokens (Doubled vocabulary from 1.2B)
* **Attention Heads**: 32 Query Heads, 8 KV Heads ($d_{\text{head}} = 64$)
* **ShortConv Window ($K$)**: 3 (rolling state buffer = 2 past tokens per channel)
* **FFN Intermediate Dimension**: 8,192 (SwiGLU)
* **RMS Norm Epsilon**: $1 \times 10^{-5}$
* **Target Hardware Footprint**: **100% resident in GPU VRAM** (~1.59 GiB on 8 GB RTX 3070, leaving >6 GiB free headroom).

## 4. Hardware Budget & Performance Roofline

* **VRAM Weight Footprint**: ~1,589 MiB.
* **Rolling Conv State**: $22 \text{ layers} \times 2 \times 2048 \times 4 \text{ bytes} = 352 \text{ KiB}$.
* **KV Cache (4K Context)**: $8 \text{ layers} \times 2 \times 8 \times 64 \times 4096 \times 2 \text{ bytes} \approx 64 \text{ MiB}$.
* **Roofline on RTX 3070 Laptop (330 GB/s)**:
  $$\frac{1.59\text{ GiB}}{330\text{ GB/s}} \approx 4.8\text{ ms/tok} \implies \mathbf{\sim 205\text{ tok/s}}$$
* **Target with Static CUDA Graph**: **$\ge 175 - 190$ tok/s**.
